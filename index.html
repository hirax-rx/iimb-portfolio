PROJECT 01 // INDEPENDENT VENTURE
Consumer Brand • Product Development • Business Strategy
Stage: Product Development & Commercial Planning

MEESU — Artisanal Eggless Tiramisu

THE CHALLENGE →

“Could I turn a familiar dessert into a commercially differentiated consumer brand for Bengaluru?”

Meesu is an independently developed eggless tiramisu brand concept for Bengaluru. What started as an interest in tiramisu became a comprehensive exercise combining actual recipe experimentation with commercial viability, consumer research and business economics.

Rather than starting with a capital-intensive storefront, I explored how a specialized dessert brand could enter the Bengaluru market through a dual-channel strategy: B2B supply to specialty third-wave cafés and targeted direct-to-consumer (D2C) curated batch drops.

✦ KEY COMMERCIAL LEARNING

“A product needs more than consumer appeal. Its positioning, economics, repeatability and distribution must work together.”

— Hiranmayi S., Meesu Venture Notebook

■ Work Conducted & Strategic Milestones

A.
Recipe Formulation & Texture Testing

Formulated and tested eggless tiramisu recipes, experimenting with mascarpone, cream cheese, whipping creams, coffee extraction composition and precise ingredient ratios for chilled stability.

B.
Product Portfolio & Flavors

Explored Classic Cocoa, Oreo Crumble, Alphonso Mango, and Thai Milk Tea variants, alongside customizable single-serve portions and sealed gift packaging.

C.
Competitor & Pricing Benchmarking

Studied competing dessert brands, specialty roasters, and premium Bengaluru cafés to pinpoint customer price thresholds and premium dessert spending habits.

D.
Dual-Channel Business Models

Developed preliminary B2B café-supply frameworks and D2C sales models, evaluating distribution viability, cold-chain transport, and potential catering expansion.

E.
Unit Economics & Margin Modeling

Investigated ingredient sourcing, portion-level costs, proposed selling prices, margin assumptions, and shelf-life production constraints.

F.
Branding & Creative Identity

Developed brand identity directions, product catalogue concepts, packaging references, and three unproduced cinematic promotional campaign scripts.

G.
Café Partnerships & Sampling

Investigated prospective café partnerships, curated tasting sessions, and structured sampling approaches for initial consumer feedback.

[FACTUAL STAGE NOTE: Product development and commercial planning. Has not launched commercially, nor achieved commercial sales or signed café contracts.]
PROJECT 02 // VENTURE CONCEPT
Advertising Technology • Market Entry • Venture Strategy
Stage: Feasibility Research & Commercial Analysis (Concluded)

KAIRO — Digital Signage & Aerial Displays

PERIOD: APRIL – JUNE 2026 • CO-DEVELOPED VENTURE CONCEPT
THE THESIS →

“An attempt to explore the next generation of physical advertising across large-scale public events.”

Kairo was conceived around importing high-resolution digital advertising displays from China and exploring aerial LED displays for concerts, sporting events and large-scale urban promotions.

The project examined international supply chain mechanics, display hardware specifications, import tariffs, and potential event-based advertising monetization models.

⚠ CRITICAL FEASIBILITY TAKEAWAY

The project was not commercially executed because the required initial capital requirement was beyond our available capital. Subsequently observing similar technologies enter the Indian market reinforced my interest in emerging advertising formats and the necessity of identifying opportunities early.

■ Strategic Investigation Scope

  • International Market & Competitor Research: Mapped international digital signage trends, competitor offerings, and Indian venue adoption readiness.
  • Hardware & Supplier Comparisons: Audited Shenzhen-based LED display manufacturers, assessing pixel pitch, brightness (nits), weight, and modular rigging requirements.
  • Import Economics & Capital Outlay: Modeled customs duties, ocean/air freight, warehousing, assembly costs, and cashflow runways.
  • Ad Revenue Models: Modeled event-day CPMs, sponsor package tiers, time-slot leasing, and multi-brand rotation revenue potential.
  • Event-Based Applications: Outlined operational blueprints for music festivals, stadium sports, and experiential brand activations.
[FACTUAL STAGE NOTE: Concept was not commercially executed. No customer validation, completed imports, live pilots, or commercial revenues achieved.]
